NyoniLine · In active development

The operating system for Zambia’s poultry economy.

Flock health, market intelligence, feed planning and daily records. Built around the farmer, not an assumption that the signal will always be there.

Discuss a NyoniLine pilot ↗

Currently in development. Ask us about a product walkthrough or a pilot discussion.

Built for a busy day

Know what happened. Decide what comes next.

A farm’s records should help with the next decision: a change in mortality, feed running short, a vaccination due or a buyer’s price. NyoniLine is being built to keep those signals close to the daily work.

The opportunity is to make useful decisions easier to see, easier to trust and easier to act on. The product brings flock, feed and finance records together rather than asking the farmer to maintain separate notebooks.

01

AI-assisted flock care

Symptom support and a chat assistant are intended to help farmers identify useful next steps and questions for a poultry professional. The tool must not claim a definitive diagnosis or replace veterinary assessment.

02

Flock and vaccination records

Track batches, daily observations, mortality, weight and vaccination schedules. A reliable history helps the farmer spot a change and gives a caretaker a clearer handover.

03

Feed formulation and planning

Record feed use, estimate what remains and plan the next order. Formulation tools need appropriate input quality and review before a recommendation is treated as suitable for a flock.

04

Finances and caretaker logs

Connect daily feed, water and flock entries with spending and sales. Caretaker records are intended to make work visible without turning every task into a separate reporting exercise.

05

Agribusiness reporting

Bring flock, feed and financial records into farm-level reports so daily entries can support planning, performance reviews and business discussions.

06

Market intelligence and marketplace workflows

Prices and trading workflows connect farm decisions to the poultry value chain. Prices need a named source, location and date; a displayed price is not a guaranteed offer.

07

Offline-first field records

Capture useful daily information when connectivity is inconsistent, with visible pending sync and resilient retry/conflict handling. Offline scope and supported devices must be tested before pilot commitments.

Designed around Zambian poultry workflows.

Daily records should connect to the business decision. Field capture, sync resilience and trustworthy advice are design requirements to validate with farmers, not just features to list.

Current stage

What to know before a pilot.

Is NyoniLine already available?+

It is in active development. Contact iMap for the current demonstration stage and possible pilot discussions.

Does the assistant diagnose disease?+

The intended role is symptom support and practical next-step guidance. Suspected disease, unusual mortality and treatment decisions require a qualified poultry professional.

Will all functions work offline?+

Offline-first capture is a design priority, not a claim that every function is offline. Market updates and some assistant functions may require connectivity; supported scope will be made explicit.
