Website privacy

A clear explanation of this website’s enquiry flow.

This page describes the marketing website, not the terms or privacy model of future iMap products.

Email enquiries

The form prepares a draft using the details you enter. It does not submit the form to an iMap website database or automatically book a meeting. Your message reaches the company only when you send it through your email application or contact us directly.

What to include

Send your name, contact details and a broad work outline. Do not include passwords, patient records, member registries, bank credentials or other sensitive records. A suitable secure exchange can be agreed later if needed.

Search and browser behaviour

Site search filters a public page list in your browser. The website does not need an account to browse. Hosting infrastructure may process ordinary request logs; email enquiries use your email provider and the company’s mailbox services.

External links and product previews

Whitewood and other external sites have their own practices. The supplied NyoniLine screen uses illustrative farm data. Lumikiza’s illustrative concept image is not a live interface or a description of a data-processing arrangement.

Questions or correction requests
