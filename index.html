Questions

Before you call.

We already have a system. Do you replace it?

Only if it needs it. We map what you have first, then fix, connect or replace only what is holding you back.

Who supports it after you leave?

We do, if you want that, and your staff are trained either way. Every system ships with documentation.

Where will our data be kept?

We agree that with you at the start. Options include hosting in Zambia and cloud services. We design with Zambia's Data Protection Act of 2021 in mind.

Can you report to our donor's format?

Yes. We build indicators, dashboards and reports to match your donor's requirements.

What does it cost?

Every project is quoted after a scoping call, because scope decides the price. The call is free.
