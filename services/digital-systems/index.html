IT & digital services

Digital systems that fit the way your team works.

A health programme, field team and growing business rarely need the same software. We start with the workflow, then build or configure the tools around it.

Who this is for

Health programmes, NGOs, public institutions and businesses with disconnected records or manual processes.

Discuss this service ↗

What you receive

  • An agreed workflow and requirements document
  • Configured or custom software with tested access controls
  • Data mappings, deployment notes and user training
  • A clear plan for support, hosting and future changes
01

Digital health platforms

Implementation and configuration of DHIS2 Aggregate and Tracker, REDCap and KoboToolbox. We work on forms, indicators, access, reporting and the data pipelines that connect collection to analysis.

02

Custom web and mobile applications

Applications for field capture, internal operations and customer services. Where offline use matters, we design the local record, sync status and conflict-handling rules rather than treating connectivity as guaranteed.

03

AI enablement and ChatOps

Assistants grounded in approved organisational guidelines, Ministry of Health manuals or custom SOPs, with permission boundaries and human review. Offline-capable models and natural-language database querying (Text-to-SQL) are scoped against your devices, data and operating risks.

04

Document intelligence and OCR

Extract structured fields from forms, receipts and scanned documents. Handwriting and clinical records require quality checks and a review queue; an uncertain extraction should not silently become a trusted record.

05

Geospatial and GIS tools

Catchment mapping, spatial analysis and interactive coverage views using tools such as QGIS and Folium. We agree the geographic source, resolution and intended decision before building the map.

06

ERP and business workflows

Configure and extend business systems for operations, logistics and reporting. Existing processes, user roles and handover documentation guide the implementation.

07

APIs and interoperability

Data adapters and controlled interfaces for legacy systems, EHR workflows and databases, including SmartCare and SmartCare Pro. Integration depends on authorised access, documented formats and the relevant system owner’s approval.

Scope questions

Before we start.

Do we need to replace our existing software?+

Not necessarily. We map what already works, then decide what to connect, repair, configure or build.

Can AI work without an internet connection?+

Some use cases can use local models. Device capacity, model quality, update processes and safeguards need to be tested for your particular workflow.
