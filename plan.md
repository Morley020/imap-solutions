

## Phase 1 — Approved positioning and information architecture update

The site now foregrounds iMap as a Zambia-based consultancy and technology delivery partner. The primary service model is seven clear pillars: Consultancy & advisory; AI & automation; IT solutions; Cybersecurity; Data, business intelligence & MERL; Telecoms & connectivity; and Software development.

The header now provides Services and Sectors discovery alongside Products, Work, About and Insights. The homepage leads with senior decision support, then presents the seven service rows, six sector pathways, delivery method, qualified proof, products and a consultation CTA. The sector pathways cover government/NGOs/civil society/parastatals, health, agriculture, education, green and blue economy, and FinTech/ecommerce.

The canonical origin is `https://imapsolutions.co.zm`. Existing service URLs are preserved through permanent Vercel redirects to the new taxonomy. The build remains dependency-free and statically generated; the route manifest and sitemap are generated from the new service set.

Phase 2 remains: create dedicated sector pages and refine deeper service content, work proof, contact topics and structured data after reviewing the Phase 1 deployment.
