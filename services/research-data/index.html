IT & digital services

Ask a useful question. Build evidence you can use.

We connect research methods, field operations and analysis so the final output can inform a decision, not just occupy a folder.

Who this is for

NGOs, donors, government programmes, research institutions, CBOs and FBOs.

Discuss this service ↗

What you receive

  • An agreed question, methodology and indicator framework
  • Quality-controlled collection and documented data handling
  • Analysis and reporting linked to the evidence
  • Optional dashboards with documented refresh and access rules
01

Programme evaluations

Baseline, midline, endline, formative, summative, process and impact evaluations. Method selection, sampling and limits are agreed before fieldwork, with a report that distinguishes evidence from inference.

02

Field data collection

Local quantitative and qualitative collection teams using digital tools. Training, supervision, consent and quality checks are included in the field plan.

03

Data cleaning and analysis

Documented validation, indicator calculation and interpretation. We make missing data and quality limitations visible instead of hiding them behind a dashboard.

04

Embedded dashboards and iMap Analytics

Translate evaluation and operational reporting into interactive views where appropriate. Indicator definitions, access permissions and refresh frequency are explicit; live data is not promised unless a working source supports it.

05

Geographic and sector reporting

Spatial and programme views for health, education, agriculture, climate and development. The map supports the question, with appropriate aggregation and privacy controls.
