IT & digital services

Protect your premises. Keep communication useful.

Security starts with the way a place is used. Communication starts with the reason a message is being sent. We design both around the people and procedures involved.

Who this is for

Businesses, homes, campuses and organisations managing premises, access or customer communications.

Discuss this service ↗

What you receive

  • A site or communication-workflow assessment
  • An installation/integration plan with clear responsibilities
  • Tested access, retention and notification settings
  • User training, documentation and maintenance terms
01

CCTV installation and maintenance

Camera placement, IP configuration and recording-system setup based on a site survey. We help reduce avoidable coverage gaps and support authorised footage review after an incident, subject to recording availability and retention.

02

Access control systems

Door controllers, RFID cards and biometric options selected against user numbers, procedures and security needs. Access and attendance records require appropriate privacy, permission and retention controls.

03

Cybersecurity and threat management

Endpoint protection, network firewalls, email safeguards, hardening and staff awareness. Security testing and incident responsibilities are agreed as part of the engagement.

04

AI governance and privacy advisory

Review use cases, policies, data handling and technical safeguards with Zambia’s Data Protection Act No. 3 of 2021 and AI-management frameworks such as ISO/IEC 42001 in view. Advisory or framework alignment is not a certification or a blanket compliance guarantee.

05

Bulk SMS and messaging gateways

Promotional messages, operational notifications and OTP workflows with controlled APIs. We scope recipient permissions, opt-outs, delivery reporting, segmentation, supplier pricing and message limits.

06

Targeted campaigns and outreach

Audience-specific customer updates, health alerts and transactional notifications. Automations have review points and suppression rules so a useful workflow does not become unwanted broadcasting.

Scope questions

Before we start.

Can CCTV eliminate every blind spot?+

A site survey helps identify coverage needs and limitations. The final camera plan, lighting and retention settings are agreed with you; no installation makes every incident observable.

Can bulk SMS connect to our existing system?+

Where your system and provider support an authorised integration, yes. We first confirm the API, message purpose, contact permissions and cost controls.
