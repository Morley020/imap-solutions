Services

Turn programme and operational data into decisions people can defend.

We connect research methods, field operations, data quality and analysis so evidence becomes useful to leadership, programmes and communities.

Who this is for

Government programmes, NGOs, donors, research institutions, civil-society organisations and businesses managing performance data.

Discuss this service ↗

What you receive

  • A question, indicator or decision framework
  • Documented collection, quality and governance approach
  • Analysis, dashboard or reporting output linked to the evidence
  • Refresh, access, interpretation and learning arrangements
01

MERL design and evaluations

Support baseline, midline, endline, formative, process and impact work with methods, indicators, sampling and limitations made explicit.

02

Data collection and quality systems

Design digital forms, supervision, validation, consent and quality checks using tools such as DHIS2, KoboToolbox and REDCap where appropriate.

03

Business intelligence and dashboards

Build decision-oriented dashboards with clear definitions, permissions, refresh expectations and visible data-quality limitations.

04

Data engineering and analysis

Clean, map, transform and analyse operational or programme data so the output is reproducible and useful beyond a single report.

05

GIS and sector intelligence

Use geographic and sector views to answer a defined question, with aggregation and privacy controls appropriate to the evidence.

Scope questions

Before we start.

Do you only build dashboards?+

No. A clear report, cleaned dataset, indicator framework or evaluation design may be the better outcome. We use dashboards when repeated decisions and updates justify them.

Can you work with donor reporting requirements?+

Yes. We can map the required definitions, templates, evidence and reporting cycle before designing the collection or analysis workflow.
