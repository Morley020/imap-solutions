Services

Make security part of how the organisation operates.

We help teams understand exposure, reduce avoidable risk and establish practical controls without pretending that any single product removes every threat.

Who this is for

Public institutions, NGOs, businesses and programme teams handling sensitive systems, personal data or public trust.

Discuss this service ↗

What you receive

  • A scoped security and privacy risk picture
  • Prioritised controls and remediation plan
  • Configured safeguards with ownership and documentation
  • Awareness, incident-readiness and review recommendations
01

Security assessment and risk planning

Map systems, users, data, dependencies and likely failure modes to create a practical, prioritised security plan.

02

Endpoint, email and network protection

Harden devices, accounts, email and network boundaries with controls matched to the organisation’s capacity and operating model.

03

Identity and access management

Clarify roles, privileged access, authentication, joiner/mover/leaver processes and review responsibilities.

04

Privacy and responsible technology

Support data-handling reviews, retention decisions and AI governance with Zambia’s Data Protection Act and relevant frameworks in view.

05

Incident readiness and awareness

Prepare practical response responsibilities, reporting routes, recovery priorities and staff guidance before an incident occurs.
