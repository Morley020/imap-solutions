About iMap

Local understanding. Connected capability.

iMap Digital Solutions is a Zambia-based consultancy and technology delivery partner helping organisations turn complex operational problems into secure, usable systems.

A partner for the decision and the delivery.

Some organisations need a clear technology direction. Others need a system, a network, better evidence or a safer way to work. iMap connects the advisory and delivery skills needed to move from the first question to a useful, documented result.

Our work is grounded in Zambia and shaped around the people, infrastructure, data and institutional responsibilities that determine whether a solution works beyond the demonstration.
