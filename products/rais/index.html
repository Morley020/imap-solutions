Approved product direction

Religious institution records, with clear responsibilities.

RAIS is the Religious Affairs Information System. Its approved direction brings membership, households, ceremonies, attendance, notices, recorded contributions and reporting into a controlled administration workspace.

The intended first step is a bounded member-management prototype, then a controlled pilot with clear institutional permissions, privacy responsibilities and support arrangements.

RAIS is not launched software. It is not a government registry, an automatic filing service, a donation wallet or a public member directory.

01

Administrative records

Scoped member and household records, controlled corrections and a protected action history.

02

Ceremonies and certificates

Verified milestone records and institution-approved certificate issue/reissue workflows.

03

Daily operations and reporting

Attendance, notices, recorded giving and permissioned reports, with future integrations gated separately.

Start with a small, useful workflow.

We are defining the prototype, required fields and access rules before bringing real member records into a pilot. Speak with iMap about the current stage and institution needs.
