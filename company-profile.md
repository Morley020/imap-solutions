# iMap Digital Solutions Limited

## Software, data and IT infrastructure for Zambia

iMap Digital Solutions Limited connects digital applications, research evidence and physical IT infrastructure for organisations working in Zambia. We start with the workflow, agree the scope, deliver the system and document the handover.

## Company and strategic partner

iMap is a joint venture between **VirTech Solutions**, established in 2016 by telecommunications and IT engineers; **Lutem Innovations**, established in 2019; and **Adino Creations**, founded in 2020. The combined work spans digital health, software, research, telecommunications and infrastructure.

**Whitewood Services (Whitewood Management Services)** is iMap’s Strategic Management Partner, distinct from the three joint-venture companies. Whitewood supports strategy implementation, governance, risk, compliance and SME growth. Lumikiza is Whitewood’s SME advisory platform, not an iMap-owned product. Learn about the partner at https://www.whitewoodms.co.zm/.

## Our vision and mission

Our vision is to build technology that enables organisations and communities to make better decisions and participate in the digital economy. Our mission is to develop practical, reliable and research-informed technology that addresses real-world work.

## Digital products

**NyoniLine — in active development.** A poultry-management product direction for Zambia connecting AI-assisted symptom support, flock records, vaccinations, feed planning, finances, caretaker logs, agribusiness reporting and market intelligence. Offline-first capture and resilient sync are priorities to validate in field use. Guidance does not replace veterinary assessment, and no public download or completed pilot results are claimed.

**Lumikiza — Whitewood partner platform.** A bridge between SME founders and experienced advisors through structured digital advisory services. Availability and engagement terms are managed by Whitewood at https://www.whitewoodms.co.zm/lumikiza.

**RAIS — approved direction.** Religious institution administration and accountable records. Prototype and controlled pilot scope are being defined; it is not launched software or a government registry.

**iMap Analytics — concept.** Operational and programme reporting shaped around useful decisions and indicator definitions. **Zambia Service Watch — in development.** Structured reporting intended to make public-service information easier to understand.
