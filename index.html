Before you call

A few things worth knowing.

Can you work with our existing systems?+

Yes. We assess what you have and decide what to connect, repair, configure or replace.

Who supports the system after handover?+

Your staff receive the agreed training and documentation. Ongoing iMap support can be included through a scoped maintenance agreement.

Where will our data be hosted?+

We agree the hosting, backup and access model with you. Local and cloud options are assessed against your requirements and relevant data-protection obligations.

Are NyoniLine and RAIS available to download?+

NyoniLine is in active development and RAIS is an approved product direction. Contact us to discuss the current stage, a demonstration or possible pilot involvement.

What will the project cost?+

The initial scoping call is free. We quote the agreed work after understanding the scope, equipment and support needs.
